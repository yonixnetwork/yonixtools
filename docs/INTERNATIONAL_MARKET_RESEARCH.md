## 0. Method, rules and limits

- **Numbers:** Every figure has a source name, URL and date. Internet-user figures come from the **DataReportal / Kepios "Digital 2026"** country reports. That series was published in late 2025 and its figures refer to **October 2025**. (DataReportal says the Digital 2026 series "relies on data up to October 2025": https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-global-overview-report.) Search results also surfaced "Digital 2027" pages that claim end-2026 figures. Today is 2026-11-14, so those were **not used**; they could not be checked and may be projections.
- **Search engine shares** are from **StatCounter Global Stats**, with the month stated. StatCounter values differ by view (all platforms, mobile, desktop) and by month. They measure page-view referrals, not query volume.
- **Tooling limit:** Direct page fetching was blocked in this session. Figures were taken from search-result excerpts of the cited pages. A spot-check against the live pages is recommended before external use.
- **Internet population ≠ demand.** Large internet populations show *potential reach* only. They do not show demand for PDF, image, video or downloader tools. Where no category evidence was found, the text says **"no evidence found"**. Competitor audience estimates (Semrush/Ahrefs) are third-party models of *competitors'* traffic. They are directional only and are **not** measurements of demand for YoniXTools.
- All URLs were accessed 2026-11-14.
